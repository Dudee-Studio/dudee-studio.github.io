Plan ahead

Create an itemFree
  1. Open Items → New item.
  2. Enter a name. If the item already exists, choose the existing suggestion.
  3. If needed, open More details and choose a category, unit, price and store.
  4. Tap Create.
Check your items before shoppingFree
  1. Open Items and work through the categories from top to bottom.
  2. Tap + for anything you need. Use −/+ to adjust the quantity.
  3. Switch to Dishes if you want to add ingredients. Your position in Items is kept.
  4. Open List when you go shopping.
Change an item’s name, price or unitFree
  1. Find the item under Items and tap its name. The pencil icon on List opens the same editor.
  2. Change the name, or open Category & unit or Price & stores.
  3. Enter your details and tap Done.
Add or change an item photoFree
  1. Open the item under Items.
  2. Expand Photo & barcode. Choose Take photo or Choose from gallery.
  3. If needed, choose Rotate / crop and confirm the crop with Apply.
  4. Close the item with Done.
Mark frequently used itemsFree
  1. Open the item and expand Pantry & favorite.
  2. Enable ★ Favorite and tap Done.
  3. Tap the star icon in Items to show only favorites.
Use shopping suggestionsFree
  1. Open List → ⋮ → Shopping suggestions.
  2. Check the suggested items and quantities. Your entered pantry stock is taken into account.
  3. Select what you need and tap the button to add the selection to your list.

A ~ quantity on an item is an estimate, not a guaranteed measure of consumption.

Set up a recurring needFree
  1. Open List → ⋮ → Shopping suggestions → Add a recurring need.
  2. Tap Add and choose an existing item.
  3. Enter the quantity, days between purchases and next needed date. Use DD.MM.YYYY.
  4. Tap Save and Done. You can also edit, pause or delete an existing rule here.

The interval restarts after you save a purchase.

Leave a short shopping noteFree
  1. Open the item under Items or use its pencil icon on List.
  2. Enter a Shopping note, such as “Unsweetened, please” or an alternative product.
  3. Tap Done. The note also appears beside the item on your list.
Take pantry stock into accountFree
  1. Open More → Pantry.
  2. Find an item and adjust the stock you have with −/+.
  3. Check the remaining quantities in your next dish or shopping suggestion.
  4. When finishing a shopping trip, you can enable Add purchases to pantry.

The app does not automatically detect consumption at home. Update your pantry stock when needed.

Dishes & occasions

Save a dish with ingredientsFree
  1. Open Dishes → Dishes → New dish.
  2. Enter a name. Under Ingredients, use + Ingredient to add items and quantities.
  3. Under Photo & group, you can organize the dish or add a picture.
  4. Tap Save.
Add a dish’s ingredients to your listFree
  1. Open Dishes → Dishes and tap the dish.
  2. Check the ingredients and adjust quantities. Pantry stock you entered has already been deducted.
  3. Tap the button to add the selected ingredients.
  4. Switch to List and check the added items.
Decide together what to eatFree locally · group sync with Pro
  1. Open Dishes → Meal ideas → Suggest a dish and choose a saved dish.
  2. Open the proposed meal idea and check the ingredient quantities.
  3. Tap Choose & add ingredients. Already selected ideas are not added twice.

Free locally. Ideas are shared in a connected group; one member’s Pro version is enough for list sync.

Reuse an “Everything for…” templateFree locally · group sync with Pro
  1. Open Dishes → Templates → New template.
  2. Enter an occasion under Everything for…, such as Game night. Select items and dishes with their quantities.
  3. Tap Save template.
  4. Open the template, check the quantities and tap Add to list.

A template saves the chosen ingredient quantities. Later recipe changes do not update it automatically.

Shopping

Add a spontaneous purchaseFree
  1. Open List.
  2. Enter the item at the bottom, for example “2x Milk, Bread”.
  3. Tap + or the keyboard’s confirmation key.
  4. Use suggestions for existing items if helpful.
Adjust quantities and tick items offFree
  1. Open List and work through the items.
  2. Use −/+ to adjust quantities. Weighted items use their saved unit.
  3. Tap the item or its checkbox once it is in your basket.
  4. Open In basket if you need to undo a checkmark.
See your total as you tick items offFree
  1. Open List. The Cost overview is at the top.
  2. Add missing prices using an item’s pencil icon and Price & stores.
  3. Tick off items and compare In basket, Still needed and Total.

The estimate uses the prices you entered. Missing prices, different checkout prices and discounts not yet entered can change the actual total.

Record a temporary offerFree
  1. Open the item and expand Limited-time offer.
  2. Enter the offer price, date range and store if applicable.
  3. Tap Done. The offer is used only during the entered date range.

The regular price is kept separately.

Apply coupons and save your shopping tripFree
  1. Tick off the items you actually bought on List.
  2. Tap Finish shopping and expand Coupons & discounts.
  3. Enter a euro or percentage discount for the shopping trip or individual items.
  4. Check To pay and tap Save shopping trip. Unchecked items stay on the list.

A coupon is recorded as a discount; the app does not redeem it with the retailer.

Share your list as textFree
  1. Open List and tap the share icon at the top.
  2. Choose an app or contact in Android’s share menu.
  3. Check the text and send it yourself.

This sends a text copy. Use a shared list for changes that stay in sync.

Organize items & stores

Create and reorder your own categoriesFree
  1. Open Items and tap Manage categories at the top. Alternatively, open More → Settings & help → Stores & prices.
  2. Enter a name under New category and tap Add category.
  3. Use the arrows to change the order, then tap Done.
  4. Assign items in the item editor under Category & unit.
Save stores and their pricesFree
  1. Open More → Settings & help → Stores & prices → Stores. Use New store to add one.
  2. Open an item → Price & stores → ＋ Store and enter its price.
  3. Choose the appropriate store beside the item on List when several are available.

Understand spending

Compare an offer with your last offer purchaseFree
  1. Find the item under Items.
  2. Read Last bought on offer with its price and purchase date. It also appears inside the item editor.
  3. Compare that recorded purchase price with the current offer.

This information comes from a finished, saved offer purchase. Without one, no previous offer price is available.

Compare this month with last monthFree
  1. Save finished shopping trips with Finish shopping → Save shopping trip.
  2. Open More → Spending → Spending.
  3. Compare This month and Last month. Open a purchase below for its details.

The overview includes only purchases you have saved in the app.

See how an item’s recorded price has changedFree
  1. Open More → Spending → Prices.
  2. Find the item and tap it.
  3. Compare recorded prices and dates in the price history.

These are your recorded prices, not automatic market monitoring.

Review spending by categoryPro version
  1. Open More → Spending → Spending.
  2. Tap Show more insights.
  3. Scroll to Spending by category and compare amounts and shares.
See which items take the largest share of spendingPro version
  1. Open More → Spending → Spending → Show more insights.
  2. Scroll to Where your money goes.
  3. Compare the amounts spent on items.

The list is ranked by spending, not by how often items are bought.

Compare spending by storePro version
  1. Open More → Spending → Spending → Show more insights.
  2. Scroll to Spending by store.
  3. Compare store totals from your saved purchases.

Plan together

Create a shared list and invite othersPro version
  1. Open More → Shop together → Start your own shared list.
  2. Enter your name and tap Start a new shared list.
  3. Tap Invite someone, then Share code.
  4. The invited person accepts the code on their phone. Create a new code for each additional person.

One valid Pro version in the group is enough for list sync. Each invite is valid once and for 48 hours. Personal scanner and detailed analysis access each require that person’s own Pro version.

Join a shared list for freeFree to join
  1. Ask your group for a valid invitation code.
  2. Open More → Shop together → Accept an invitation.
  3. Enter your name and the code, then tap Join.

Plan with a partner, friends, housemates, family or another group. Sync requires an internet connection.

Return to your group after changing phonesFree to join
  1. Before changing phones, open Account & backup in your shared list and choose Back up with Google account.
  2. On the new phone, open More → Shop together → Return after switching phones.
  3. Tap Sign in with Google and choose the same Google account.

Linking Google protects your membership. Also export local dishes and price histories separately.

Leave a group or manage a memberFree to join
  1. Open More → Shop together.
  2. Under Members, the manager can remove a member or choose Make manager.
  3. To leave, choose Manage list → Leave list → Yes, leave.

“Delete the list for everyone” ends the shared list for every member. Choose it only if that is what you intend.

Scanner & search

Add an item by barcodePro version
  1. Open List and tap Scan barcode at the bottom.
  2. Scan the product’s barcode. A known item is added to your list.
  3. Check new product data. If the barcode is unknown, enter a name.
  4. For an existing item without a photo: open it → Photo & barcode → Scan.

Product data and images are imported from Open Food Facts only when available. Online lookups require internet.

Find an item onlinePro version
  1. Open Items → Search items online using the search icon at the top.
  2. Enter a search term and tap Search.
  3. Tap a result to add it to your list. Then check its name, photo and category.

Results come from Open Food Facts, not a retailer’s current offer feed.

Settings & backup

Export and import your dataFree
  1. Open More → Settings & help → Back up data → Export to file.
  2. Choose a safe location for the backup file.
  3. To restore, open Import from file, choose whether to import pantry stock and the shopping list, then tap Choose file.
  4. Select your backup file and check the import result.

A backup can contain your photos and shopping data. Store it appropriately.

Choose German or English for the appFree
  1. Open More → Settings & help.
  2. Tap Language and choose Deutsch or English.
  3. Choose System language to follow your device’s language again.

Your own item, dish and store names stay as you entered them.

Change the theme and soundsFree
  1. Open More → Settings & help → Appearance & sounds.
  2. Tap Color scheme and choose Light, Dark or System, plus your preferred palette.
  3. Use Sounds to turn audio feedback on or off.
Buy or restore the Pro versionFree
  1. Open More → Settings & help → Sharing & Pro → Pro / restore purchases.
  2. Check the one-time price shown by Google Play and choose Buy Pro if wanted.
  3. For an existing purchase, use the same Play account and tap Restore purchases / reload.

No subscription. Google Play shows the current price, which can vary by country.

Delete your group accountFree to join
  1. Open More → Shop together → Remove account.
  2. Choose Delete account and read the confirmation.
  3. Confirm with Yes, delete account if you want to remove the account and memberships.

The app explains the effect on lists you manage. Local data on this phone is kept. Without the app, contact info@dudee-studio.com.

Show your list on the home screenFree
  1. Touch and hold an empty area on your Android home screen and open Widgets.
  2. Find Abgehakt or Einkaufsliste and place the widget.
  3. Open the app from the widget when you want to change more details.

Home screen menu labels vary by Android device.

Community catalog · preview 1.1

Import an item from the community catalogPreview 1.1 · Pro version
  1. Once the catalog is enabled, open Items → New item and enter a name.
  2. Tap Search the community catalog · Pro version and read the online-use notice before your first search.
  3. Choose a suitable result with Apply.
  4. Check your own copy, adjust the category and tap Create.

Version 1.1 preview: live activation is still pending. The catalog contains contributions from app users; reported prices are not guaranteed current prices.

Choose to share your own item with the communityPreview 1.1 · free
  1. Once the catalog is enabled, open your own item → Share items publicly.
  2. Read the notice and consent if you want to publish. Automatic sharing starts off.
  3. Opt in to sharing your own photo or a price only if you want to.
  4. Tap Publish now.

Version 1.1 preview. Publishing does not require the Pro version. Personal purchase history is not shared; publish only content you have the right to share.

Withdraw a shared item or report a contributionPreview 1.1 · free
  1. Once enabled, open your published item → Share items publicly → Withdraw.
  2. Check for the Shared item withdrawn confirmation.
  3. Use Report on an inappropriate catalog search result.

Version 1.1 preview. Withdrawal removes the publication; copies already imported by other users remain.
