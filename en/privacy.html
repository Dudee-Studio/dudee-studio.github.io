At a glance

Without group sync, the community item catalog or online search, the app stores its content locally on your device. Depending on your device and account settings, Android may transfer app data through system backups or when changing devices. Pro purchase entitlements are excluded from this backup; they are restored through Google Play. Only when you use a shared list are the list, items, meal ideas and occasion templates also stored on a server (Google Firebase, data centers in the EU), so other members can see them. For barcode scanning and online search, the app queries the free Open Food Facts database for product information. The app has no advertising, analytics or tracking services.

Data processed on your device

  • Shopping lists, items, dishes, pantry quantities, prices and completed purchases that you enter yourself.
  • Photos taken with the camera or selected from your gallery to illustrate items or dishes. The app stores a smaller copy in its own storage. It does not otherwise read your gallery.
  • Your store name, if you set one.

This data is held in the app’s private storage on your device. Other apps cannot access it.

Group sync (shared list)

Group sync is optional and only starts when you create or join a shared list.

  • Account: The app creates an anonymous account, consisting of a random identifier without an email address or password.
  • Data sent to the server: your display name within the list, the shopping list, items (name, department, unit, prices, stores, pantry quantity, barcode and shopping notes), meal ideas and occasion templates including selected dish names and ingredient quantities, completed purchases, and reduced copies of your own item photos (400 pixels). Other dishes, full-size photos and price histories remain on your device.
  • Who can see it: only members of this list. New members can join only through an invitation code valid for 48 hours and redeemable once. The server enforces this.
  • Secure with a Google account (optional): If you link your account to your Google account, Firebase Authentication stores your email address and Google account identifier so you can return to your lists after reinstalling the app or changing devices.
  • Provider and location: Google Firebase, provided by Google Ireland Limited. Lists are stored in Cloud Firestore in Europe. Google may also process account data (Firebase Authentication) in the USA, based on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Transfers are encrypted. Google acts as a processor.
  • Abuse prevention: Firebase App Check (Google Play Integrity) identifies the app to the server as a genuine app installed from Google Play. Google Play checks the device and app; no list content is transferred for this check.
  • Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR: the shared list is the feature you request.

Community item catalog (from version 1.1)

Use is optional. Before the first access, the app explains online processing and asks for consent. Searching and importing require the Pro version; publishing items and withdrawing your own publications are free.

  • Search: Your search text and technically necessary connection data, including your IP address, are sent to Google Firebase. Search matches the beginning of item names, regardless of capitalization and accents. We do not store search text as a search history.
  • Account: The catalog uses the same Firebase app account as group sync. If none exists, an anonymous account is created. You do not need a shared list or Google sign-in.
  • Public sharing: You can publish items only after explicit consent. The item name, department and unit become public to other signed-in app users. If separately selected, your own product photo (up to 400 pixels) and a reported price with an optional store and report date are also shared. The technical account identifier is used to assign and delete your publications; your display name and email address are not published with the item. Shopping notes, shopping lists, pantry quantities, purchase frequencies, personal purchase dates and price histories are not published.
  • Automatic sharing: Off by default. After consenting, you can enable sharing of future items you create, without photos or prices. Existing items are not published retroactively. You can turn this off under Settings → Back up data → Community catalog and sharing.
  • Image rights: Share only your own product photos without people, receipts, addresses or other private information, and only content for which you have the necessary rights. By publishing, you allow Dudee Studio to display the content in the catalog and other users to import the item data and photo into their shopping lists. Images from Open Food Facts are not republished as your own photos.
  • Independent copies: Imported items are independent and editable. Withdrawal removes the item and its photo from the catalog, but does not delete copies already imported by other users.
  • Deletion and withdrawal: You can withdraw individual publications in the app. Deleting your account removes your catalog items and photos before the account is deleted. Without the app, contact info@dudee-studio.com. Publications remain available until withdrawal or account deletion. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete them. If needed, link your anonymous account to Google to retain access after reinstalling. Publication is limited to 20 items per account.
  • Reports: Reporting an item sends its identifier, the reporting account’s technical identifier and a timestamp to Dudee Studio. Reports are not public; they support reviewing and removing prohibited content. Reports are deleted no later than 90 days after processing is completed.
  • Provider and location: Google Firebase, with the provider, transfers and technical safeguards described under group sync above. Prices are user reports and are not a guarantee of current store prices.
  • Legal bases: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for requested online searches and imports; Article 6(1)(a) GDPR for optional publication and automatic sharing. Consent can be withdrawn for the future by disabling automatic sharing and withdrawing publications. Article 6(1)(f) GDPR applies to abuse reports and their handling, to protect users and the catalog.

Barcode scanner and online search (Open Food Facts)

  • The scanner is provided by Google Play services. The app receives only the scanned barcode number, not camera access.
  • The app sends the barcode number or your search term, and optionally a store name, to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org, Open Food Facts, France) to request the product name, brand, category and image. Like any web server, Open Food Facts sees your IP address. No other data is sent.
  • Open Food Facts data and images are available under open licenses (ODbL and CC BY-SA respectively); the app credits the source.

Export, import and sharing

  • Export to file writes your data, including photos, to a location you choose.
  • Import from file reads such a file.
  • Share list and Share code pass text to an app you select, such as a messenger.

In these cases, you decide where the data goes.

Android backups

If Android backup is enabled for your Google account, Android may back up the app’s data in encrypted form and restore it on a new device. You control this in Android settings. We have no access to these backups.

Deletion

  • On your device: Delete individual entries in the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings deletes all its data from the device.
  • On the server: In the app, open ⋮ → Group sync → Delete account. This leaves all shared lists and deletes your account. If you manage a list, management passes to the next member. If there are no other members, the list, items, purchases and photos are deleted. Group sync also offers “Leave list” and “Dissolve list for everyone”.
  • Without the app, request deletion by emailing info@dudee-studio.com.

Pro purchase through Google Play

The Pro version is an optional one-time purchase. Google Play processes payment through your Play account under its own privacy notices. The app receives the purchase status, product identifier, purchase signature and purchase token to verify, unlock and acknowledge the purchase. It does not receive payment details such as credit card numbers. The verified unlock status is stored locally so the Pro version also works offline. A successful purchase query updates this status, for example after a refund. The legal basis for processing needed to provide the purchase is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.
